# Language policy
**Status:** Accepted · 2026-11-21 · Applies to all four repositories.
- Engineering: English, including README, CHANGELOG, architecture, ADRs, technical specs, code/comments, identifiers, automated tests, prompts, skills, Copilot instructions, development conventions and technical reports.
- Business: Spanish, including RF/RNF, product and sprint backlogs, user stories, acceptance criteria, functional specifications and functional test cases/scenarios.
- Academic deliverables, prototypes and user-facing UI: Spanish.
- Domain glossary: bilingual; preserve existing technical identifiers until a change is approved.
- Mixed HU specs keep business sections in Spanish and technical contract/verification sections in English. Functional Gherkin examples remain Spanish business scenarios, not executable test code.
- On-demand Spanish translations of engineering documents support learning. Identify original path, source revision and translation date. They are reference copies, not an alternative canonical source for implementation. Do not maintain a parallel translated repository or wait until project end to support learning.
- Preserve RF/RNF/HU/CA/test IDs. Escalate semantic ambiguity rather than silently translating domain rules.
